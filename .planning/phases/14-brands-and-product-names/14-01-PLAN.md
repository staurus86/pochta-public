<objective>
Fix two brand-detection regressions:

1. **BRAND-02 — Subject priority**: Brands detected in `classification.detectedBrands` but appearing ONLY in the email Subject (not body/attachment) are silently dropped by the P15 and P18 grounding gates in `analyzeEmail()`. Fix: compute a `subjectGroundedBrands` Set before the P15 gate and exempt those brands from both gates.

2. **BRAND-03 — Short alias rejection**: Both `DetectionKB.detectBrands` (detection-kb.js) and the local `detectBrands` function (email-analyzer.js) lack a minimum-alias-length guard. A 1-2 char alias in the KB (e.g. `"av"`, `"hp"`) triggers a word-boundary match anywhere in the email. Fix: reject single-token aliases of length <= 2 in both paths. 3-char aliases (e.g. `"abb"`) are kept — they are legitimate abbreviations for real brands.

Purpose: Increase brand `present` (subject-only brands were lost) and brand `noise_free` (short-alias false positives removed). Current baseline_v3: `present=0.65`, `noise_free=0.5833`.

Output: Modified `email-analyzer.js`, `detection-kb.js`, mirrored to `.railway-deploy/`, new test file `tests/batch-14-fixes.test.js`.
</objective>
